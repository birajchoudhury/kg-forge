Engineering Teams

The Engineering Team is responsible for building our core platform. The engineering team works closely with the Product Team to deliver features.

Our Data Engineering Team handles all data pipelines. The data engineering team is part of the larger engineering organization.

The Platform Engineering Team maintains infrastructure. Platform engineering team ensures system reliability and scalability.

Python is our primary programming language. We use Python for data processing, web development, and automation. The Python programming language is versatile and powerful.

Neo4j serves as our graph database. We chose Neo4j for its excellent graph querying capabilities. Neo4j Graph Database stores all our entity relationships.

The Product Team defines requirements and roadmap. Product team works with engineering to prioritize features.
